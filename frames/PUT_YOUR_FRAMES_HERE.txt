Drop your 300 ezgif-exported frames directly into this folder, named:

  ezgif-frame-001.png
  ezgif-frame-002.png
  ezgif-frame-003.png
  ...
  ezgif-frame-300.png

That matches the defaults in assets/js/config.js (prefix
"ezgif-frame-", 3-digit padding, .png extension).

IMPORTANT: if you exported JPG instead of PNG from ezgif, open
assets/js/config.js and change extension: '.png' to extension: '.jpg'
to match — a mismatched extension is the #1 reason the loading bar
gets stuck at 0%.

Delete this file once your frames are in place (it won't break
anything if you leave it, it's just not used by the site).
